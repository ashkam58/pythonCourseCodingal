# Activity 1: Explore Chatbots - Solution & Class Guide

## 📌 Activity Overview
- **Title:** Explore Chatbots
- **Lesson:** Meet the World of Prompts
- **Short Description:** In this activity, you will explore how different chatbots answer the same question in unique ways. By comparing their responses, you'll discover how prompts can lead to fun and surprising answers!

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open the Slides Link
1. Open the [Google Slides Template](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1QZ60e6tdnrvU8lEyXZxfQw-9PyPc96MNjhXnIH1xSwk/edit?usp=sharing).
2. The file is view-only. Click **File > Make a copy > Entire presentation** to create your own editable copy.
3. Name your presentation: `[Your Name] - Explore Chatbots Activity`.

### Step 2: Open the AI Chatbots
Open two or more of the following chatbots in separate browser tabs:
- **ChatGPT**: [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)
- **Google Gemini**: [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
- **Anthropic Claude**: [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)
- **Microsoft Copilot**: [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)

### Step 3: Sign In / Access the Models
- Log in to your accounts or use the free guest access where available.
- Locate the chat input box at the bottom of the screen where prompts are typed.

### Step 4: Enter a Prompt
Type one of the experimental prompts into each chatbot and hit Enter:
- **Fun Fact Prompt:** `"Tell me a fun fact about space in 2 sentences."`
- **Story Prompt:** `"Write a short story about a penguin who wants to be an astronaut."`
- **Riddle Prompt:** `"Give me a riddle for kids with 3 answer choices."`
- **Coding Prompt:** `"Write Game Lab code to make a rocket move upward."`

### Step 5: Compare Responses
Copy and paste the responses from each chatbot into the comparison table below. Notice differences in length, tone, emojis, and detail.

### Step 6: Record, Analyze, and Share
Answer the reflective questions:
1. What answers are the same?
2. What answers are different?
3. Which chatbot answer did you like the most, and why?

## 💡 Reflection & Discussion Questions (Solutions)

### Q1: What answers were the same or similar?
- **Answer:** All chatbots followed the constraint of writing about space, understood the requested 2-sentence limitation, and provided real astronomical facts. They all used friendly, accessible language suitable for students.

### Q2: What answers were different?
- **Answer:** Each chatbot selected completely different facts! ChatGPT chose the comparison of stars vs. sand grains; Gemini focused on the vacuum of sound and Venus's heat; Claude picked an unusual fact about an interstellar molecular cloud; Copilot highlighted lunar footprints and the absence of atmospheric erosion. The tone ranged from playful (Claude) to scientific (Gemini).

### Q3: Which chatbot answer did you like the most, and why?
- **Sample Answer:** *"I liked Gemini the most because it explained why sound cannot travel in space (vacuum) and debunked a common myth about Mercury being the hottest planet. It taught me two real science facts in just two clear sentences."*
