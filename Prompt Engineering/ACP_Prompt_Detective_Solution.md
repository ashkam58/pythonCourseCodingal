- **Project Title:** Be a Prompt Detective
- **Module:** Prompt Engineering
- **Lesson:** Meet the World of Prompts
- **Goal:** To help students practice writing, refining, and comparing prompts so they understand how wording directly shapes AI responses.

## 📖 The Detective Mission Story
> *Imagine you are a detective of AI chatbots! Your mission is to interrogate two different chatbots with the exact same questions and investigate how their answers change. By refining your prompts with clues and precision, you'll uncover how to extract sharper, more interesting, and accurate responses.*

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Instructions & Solution

### Step 1: Detective Setup
- **Chosen Chatbot 1:** ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- **Chosen Chatbot 2:** Google Gemini (Google)

### Step 2: The Three Detective Test Prompts

#### Prompt 1 (Fun Fact Prompt):
> *"Tell me 3 fun facts about the Moon."*

| Chatbot | Full Response | Detective Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | 1. **It's drifting away:** The Moon moves about 3.8 cm farther from Earth every year.<br>2. **Extreme temperatures:** During lunar day it reaches 250°F (120°C), but at night it plummets to -208°F (-130°C).<br>3. **Moonquakes:** The Moon experiences quakes caused by the gravitational pull of Earth! | Detailed explanations, included units of measurement (both Fahrenheit and Celsius), structured with bold bullet headers. |
| **Gemini** | 1. **You weigh much less:** Because lunar gravity is 1/6th of Earth's, a 60-pound kid would weigh only 10 pounds there!<br>2. **Footprints last forever:** There is no wind or water erosion, so astronaut tracks stay preserved for millions of years.<br>3. **The Moon is not round:** It is actually shaped like an egg or lemon due to Earth's gravitational pull. | Focused on relatable facts for young students (body weight, footprints), clear and visual facts. |

### Step 3: Prompt Refinement Experiment

To see how adding constraints and specificity sharpens the AI's response, we refine **Prompt 1**:

- **Original Prompt:**
  > *"Tell me 3 fun facts about the Moon."*
- **Refined Prompt:**
  > *"Tell me 3 fun facts about how astronauts lived and did daily activities on the Moon during the Apollo missions."*

#### Side-by-Side Output Comparison:

| Original Answer (General) | Refined Answer (Specific & Detailed) |
|---|---|
| Mentioned general astronomical trivia: gravity (1/6th), moonquakes, and temperature swings. | 1. **Bouncing was the best transport:** Because of their bulky 180-pound suits and low gravity, astronauts found the "bunny hop" or kangaroo skip much easier and faster than normal walking.<br>2. **Moon dust smelled like gunpowder:** When astronauts returned inside the lunar module and took off their helmets, the abrasive lunar dust clinging to their suits smelled distinctly like spent fireworks or gunpowder.<br>3. **Eating with spoons was messy:** Food had to be rehydrated with warm water using a gun-like dispenser, and water droplets would float away if squeezed too quickly! |

#### Detective Finding on Refinement:
- The original prompt yielded textbook facts about the Moon's physics.
- The refined prompt forced both AI models to focus specifically on **human daily survival, sensory experiences (smell, movement, food)**, resulting in much more exciting and immersive information!

## 📝 Student Reflection (4–5 Sentences)

Here is the completed reflection answering what was learned during this detective experiment:

> **What I Learned as a Prompt Detective:**
> *"Through this activity, I discovered that AI chatbots rely heavily on the clarity and detail of the instructions we give them. When I asked broad questions, both ChatGPT and Gemini gave general encyclopedia answers; however, when I refined my prompt to specify Apollo astronaut activities, the answers became much more vivid and captivating. I also noticed that ChatGPT tends to be slightly more dramatic in storytelling, while Gemini provides practical analogies like weighing less on the Moon. Overall, I learned that being specific, stating limits, and asking focused questions turns AI from an ordinary search engine into a powerful creative partner."*
