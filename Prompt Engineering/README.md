# Prompt Engineering - Lesson: Meet the World of Prompts

Welcome to the **Prompt Engineering** module! In this lesson, students are introduced to prompts as instructions given to AI chatbots. Students explore multiple chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) and observe how wording changes the quality, tone, and accuracy of answers.

8. **Question refinement**

## 🔍 Topics in Detail

### 1. What is a Prompt?
A prompt is like giving directions or asking a clear question. It tells a computer or chatbot exactly what you want.
- *Basic / Vague Example:* "space stuff"
- *Clear Prompt Example:* "What are three cool facts about stars?"

### 2. Prompt Writing
Writing a good prompt is like being a super-question-asker!
**Tips for strong prompts:**
- **Be specific**: State exact details, limits, or formats.
- **Use clear words**: Avoid slang or confusing sentence structures.
- **Ask one thing at a time**: Don't overwhelm the model with multiple unrelated questions.
- **Be polite & direct**: "Can you explain how dogs smell with their noses?"

### 3. Why Prompts Matter
Clear prompts = better answers! Think of prompts like game rules. If the rules are clear, everyone plays happily. If not, things get messy.

### 4. Prompt Variations
You can ask the same question in many ways to get fun, different answers:
- *"Tell me about space."* (Broad overview)
- *"What are 3 amazing facts about planets?"* (Structured, concise)
- *"Imagine you're an astronaut—what do you see?"* (Creative roleplay)

### 5. Chatbot Exploration
Different chatbots are like different friends—each explains things in their own unique style. Try asking the same question to a few chatbots and see how their answers change.

### 6. Different Chatbots
- **ChatGPT (OpenAI)**: Great at conversations, creative writing, step-by-step reasoning, and coding.
- **Google Gemini**: Highly up-to-date, connected with Google services, clear structured summaries.
- **Anthropic Claude**: Thoughtful, natural conversational tone, safe, excellent writing nuance.
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Integrated with Bing search, provides citations and concise factual answers.

### 7. Response Comparison
When comparing chatbot answers, you act like a detective:
- What is the **same** across all responses?
- What is **different** (tone, length, depth, formatting)?
- Which answer is the most helpful for your specific goal?

### 8. Question Refinement
Making your question better = sharper answers! Start simple, then add details:
- *Basic:* "Tell me about animals."
- *Refined:* "Which zoo animal has the weirdest sleeping habit?"

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson, students will have:
- Learned what a prompt is and understood its purpose.
- Practiced writing simple, clear, and focused prompts.
- Observed how wording directly influences the quality and creativity of responses.
- Compared outputs across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot.
- Refined initial questions to receive sharper, higher-quality answers.
- Gained curiosity about using AI prompts for problem-solving, storytelling, and coding.

---

## ⏱️ Lesson Agenda (45–60 mins)

1. **Warm-Up Discussion (5 mins)**:
   - Ask students: *"If you want help with homework, is it better to say 'help me' or 'help me solve question 2 step by step'?"*
   - Connect this to how AI models need clear instructions (prompts).
2. **Introduction to Prompts (5–7 mins)**:
   - Define a prompt: *"A prompt is an instruction we give to AI."*
   - Discuss vague vs. clear prompts.
3. **Exploring Chatbots (15–20 mins)**:
   - Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot.
   - Test the same prompt across chatbots and complete the comparison table.
4. **Prompt Experiments (10–12 mins)**:
   - Test variations (fun fact, story, riddle, coding).
5. **Reflection & Sharing (8–10 mins)**:
   - Discuss which chatbot gave the funniest, most factual, or most useful answer.
6. **Wrap-Up (2–3 mins)**:
   - Reinforce: Clear prompts = better answers.
   - Preview upcoming lessons: Using prompts to write code!
